Lemma 89 Softmax Lipschitz bound

l1Norm Let \(\varphi = \mathrm{softmax}\colon \mathbb {R}^n \to \Delta ^n\). For any \(x, y \in \mathbb {R}^n\),

\[ \| \varphi (x) - \varphi (y)\| _1 \leq 2\, \| x - y\| _\infty . \]

Lemma 90 Accumulation increment bound; \(\mathcal{A}_+\)

attention_bounded_away def:accumulation Let \(\varphi \) be a positive simplex map with bounded inputs. For any sequence \(\{ a_t\} \) with \(a_t(\tau ) \in [\alpha , 1-\alpha ]\) for some \(\alpha {\gt} 0\) (guaranteed by positivity of \(\varphi \) with bounded inputs; for softmax, Theorem 25 gives an explicit bound), the accumulation rule gives

\[ |\sigma _{t+1}(\tau ) - \sigma _t(\tau )| = O(1/t) \]

for each \(\tau \in X\). The implied constant depends on \(\alpha \) and \(N_0\) but not on the specific sequence \(\{ a_t\} \).

Lemma 91 Finite argmax crossings under convergent perturbation

argmaxGap def:retrieval, thm:capture_argmax Let \(\{ q_t\} \) be a sequence in \(\mathbb {R}^d\) converging to \(q^*\) with \(\sum _t \| q_{t+1} - q_t\| {\lt} \infty \). Let \(E \in \mathbb {R}^{n \times d}\) be a fixed embedding such that \(\operatorname {argmax}_\tau (q^*)^\top E(\tau )\) is unique. Then there exists \(T_0\) such that for all \(t {\gt} T_0\),

\[ \operatorname {argmax}_\tau \, q_t^\top E(\tau ) = \operatorname {argmax}_\tau \, (q^*)^\top E(\tau ). \]

The argmax changes at most \(T_0\) times.

Proof ▼
